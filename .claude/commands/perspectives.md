Analyze the following statement or question and provide diverse perspectives:

**Topic:** $ARGUMENTS

## Your Task

1. **Identify 4-6 relevant perspectives** - Think about who would have valuable, distinct viewpoints on this topic. Consider:
   - Domain experts (researchers, practitioners)
   - Skeptics and advocates
   - Different stakeholder roles (user, developer, reviewer)
   - Practical vs theoretical viewpoints

2. **For each perspective**, provide:
   - **Role/Background**: Who they are and why their view matters
   - **Position**: Their stance on the topic (1-2 sentences)
   - **Key Arguments**: 2-3 bullet points with their reasoning
   - **Concerns/Caveats**: What they'd warn about

3. **Synthesis**: After all perspectives, provide:
   - Common ground (what most agree on)
   - Key tensions (where they disagree)
   - Questions to resolve before deciding

Be specific and grounded. Use concrete examples relevant to the topic rather than generic advice.
